Tamarack Trail

September 2026 · 4 days · 52 km / 32.5 miles

Route Day 1 Day 2 Day 3 Day 4

This is a small blog post about my four-day backpacking trip on the Tamarack Trail in September 2026, starting at Rowe trailhead and finishing at Red Rock Canyon.

Route

The route includes Mount Lineham summit, a Sage Pass out-and-back, and Avion Ridge, with nights at Lone Lake, Upper Twin Lake, and Goat Lake.

These distances and climbing totals are rough estimates, including the side trips.

References

Route descriptions and trip reports for the Tamarack traverse and its side trips. These published routes do not all match this itinerary: my Day 3 crossed Avion Ridge rather than taking the standard Snowshoe Trail connection from Twin Lakes. The distances and climbing totals above remain approximate, not GPS measurements.

  • Parks Canada: hiking in Waterton Lakes National Park — official hiking information, trail distances, and links to current conditions and closures.
  • Simon Ennals: Tamarack Trail — a multi-day trip guide and planning reference.
  • Hiking Project: Tamarack Hike — an overview of the standard traverse and its campsites, described in the opposite direction, from Red Rock Canyon to Rowe Lakes trailhead.
  • Hiking With Par: Mount Lineham and Rowe Lakes — a summit trip report with a route map and details of the approach from the Rowe/Tamarack trailhead. Its full day-hike totals include lake detours, so they are not directly comparable to my Day 1.
  • Peaks and Streams: Sage Pass Peak and Sage Senior — details of the approach to Sage Pass from the Twin Lakes trail. The report continues to peaks beyond the pass; my side trip was only to the pass and back.
  • Hiking Project: Goat Lake Trail and Snowshoe Trail — descriptions of the connected trails between Goat Lake and Red Rock Canyon, useful for understanding the Day 4 exit.
